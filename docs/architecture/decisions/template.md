## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and forces that make this decision
architecturally significant. Keep the context factual and avoid presenting the
preferred option as inevitable.

## Decision

State the commitment in direct, active language. Explain its scope and make
important exclusions explicit.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative name

Explain why this was plausible and why it was not selected in the current
context. Include meaningful alternatives, not intentionally weak options.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Describe benefits created by the decision.

### Negative

- Describe costs, risks, and constraints the project accepts.

### Neutral

- Describe operational or organizational effects that are neither inherently
  positive nor negative.

## Assumptions and revisit triggers

- Record assumptions that support the current decision.
- Identify measurable changes that should cause the team to evaluate it again.

## Validation

Link to code, automated tests, configuration, or guides that demonstrate the
decision is implemented. Do not claim enforcement that does not exist.
